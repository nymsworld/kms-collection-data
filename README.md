# KMS Collection data

The card data behind [KMS Collection](https://collect.kantomisplay.cc), a free card collection tracker: every set,
card and sealed product of Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering and Yu-Gi-Oh!, with TCGplayer market prices, rebuilt
every night by `scripts/build-data.mjs` and published at https://kms-collection-data.pages.dev/data/.

Sources: the Kanto Misplay Society price database (Card.api), tcgcsv.com, and api.carddex.dev.
